3. ANNOTATION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• YES only when the supplied evidence sentence supports a relation between the declared source and target. Mere co-occurrence is not enough.
• NO for study aims, trial design, title-only questions, search terms, eligibility criteria, group membership, and marker/entity mismatch.
• UNCERTAIN is retained as a relation hypothesis but excluded from asserted-edge export.
• NEGATED evidence is retained as counter-evidence (e.g., NO_EFFECT_ON) and excluded from positive asserted-edge export.
• direction_valid=NO means the sentence contains a related fact but the declared source→target order or entity type is not suitable for that graph edge.
• polarity_sign and assertion_status are independent: NEGATIVE means a decreasing/inhibitory effect, not a false statement.
